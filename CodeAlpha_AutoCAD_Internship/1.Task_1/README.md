Create a 2D floor plan of a residential house (minimum 2 bedrooms, kitchen, living room, and bathroom), with proper dimensions, wall thickness, and labels.

## Design Summary
- **Layout type:** Two-bedroom residential unit organized around a central open courtyard.
- **Bedrooms:** Two bedrooms, each with direct ensuite bathroom access.
- **Flex spaces:** Two additional adjoining rooms (usable as study/dressing rooms) between the bedrooms.
- **Living areas:** Open living room with a semi-circular seating alcove, opening onto a covered veranda.
- **Kitchen:** Positioned along the service core adjacent to the bathrooms and utility area, to keep plumbing runs short.
- **Courtyard:** Central landscaped, open-to-sky courtyard bringing daylight and ventilation into the core of the plan and separating the private bedroom wing from the shared living/kitchen zone.
- **Circulation:** Direct external access from the bedroom wing, living room, and kitchen/utility side.

## Drafting Notes
- Walls drawn with consistent thickness and correct door swing clearances.
- Furniture layout included for spatial clarity (beds, sofas, kitchen counters, bathroom fixtures).
- Drawing organized on separate layers (walls, furniture, dimensions, text/labels) per standard CAD practice.
